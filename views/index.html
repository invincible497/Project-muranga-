How HudumaDesk Works

A simple, transparent process from submission to resolution

📝

Submit a Request

Fill in a simple form — describe the issue, pick your sub-county, and optionally attach a photo.

🔔

Get a Ticket Number

Receive a unique MRG ticket ID instantly. Use it to track your request's progress anytime.

⚙️

We Take Action

County administrators review, assign priority, and dispatch field teams to address your issue.

📊

Track in Real Time

Watch your request move from Pending → In Progress → Resolved on your dashboard.

🔍

Public Tracking

No login needed to track a ticket. Share your ticket ID and anyone can follow the progress.

📈

County Analytics

Transparent analytics show how many issues are resolved per sub-county, building public trust.
