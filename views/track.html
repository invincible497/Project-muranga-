Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my ticket ID?

Your ticket ID was shown immediately after submitting your request and also sent to your dashboard. It starts with MRG- followed by the year and a 4-digit number.

How long does it take to resolve a request?

Resolution time varies by category and complexity. Critical/safety issues are prioritised. You'll see status updates here and in your dashboard notifications.

What do the status badges mean?

Pending — received, awaiting review. In Progress — being actively addressed. Resolved — issue has been fixed. Rejected — outside county jurisdiction or duplicate.
